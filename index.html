About

Background &
Academic Profile

I am an Assistant Professor in Natural Language Processing, with a strong background in machine learning, multilingual NLP, and AI for education. My work focuses on developing human-centered intelligent systems that address real-world challenges in learning, communication, and language technology. My research particularly emphasizes multilingual representation learning, emotion recognition, and the use of generative AI to support inclusive and personalized educational applications.

I have extensive experience in teaching, curriculum development, and supervising student research across a wide range of subjects, including Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, Data Science, Language Technology, and Large Language Models. Over the years, I have taught at multiple institutions and have contributed to the design and delivery of courses that integrate theoretical foundations with practical applications.

My broader research vision centers on building scalable, interpretable, and socially meaningful AI systems. I am particularly interested in advancing NLP for low-resource languages, developing emotionally intelligent conversational technologies, and designing AI-driven educational tools that enhance student engagement and learning outcomes. Alongside my teaching and research, I am committed to mentoring students and junior academics, contributing to applied research initiatives, and fostering interdisciplinary collaboration.

BSc AI · WBAI027-05 · University of Groningen · not offered 2026–27
Language Technology Practical

A 6-week hands-on practical on QA and conversational systems for BSc Artificial Intelligence students who have completed the NLP or LLM course: transformer-based question answering, retrieval-augmented generation, evaluation of QA and RAG systems, task-oriented dialogue, and multi-agent architectures. Built around a single, continuously developed project with benchmarked weekly deliverables.

BSc AI · WBAI068-05 · University of Groningen
Large Language Models

A 7-week Bachelor course that builds on the NLP course: transformer architectures and scaling, pretraining, instruction fine-tuning and RLHF, efficiency, prompting, retrieval-augmented generation, and agentic and multi-agent systems. Covers both the theoretical foundations of modern LLMs and their practical deployment in real-world NLP applications. Lecture materials available.

BSc AI · WBAI059-05 · University of Groningen
Natural Language Processing

An 8-week, 10-lecture BSc course spanning the NLP stack: from n-gram language models and word embeddings through CNNs, LSTMs, transformers and fine-tuning to machine translation and RAG-based question answering. Built around a continuous project with baselines, ablations and evaluation at every stage, with custom slide decks and coding notebooks.

Course Highlight
RAG-Based QA Assistant
Cumulative Student Project

In the Language Technology Practical, students develop a single QA and dialogue system over six weeks, with each week's deliverable building directly on the last: from a baseline QA model to a multi-agent architecture.

  • Baseline & Generative QA (SQuAD, EM/F1)
  • RAG Pipeline (FAISS, SentenceTransformers)
  • Evaluation with RAGAS & BERTScore
  • Task-Oriented Dialogue System
  • Planner/Solver Multi-Agent Extension
